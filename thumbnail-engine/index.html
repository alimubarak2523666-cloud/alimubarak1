    Research notes · October 2026

    What makes a thumbnail work

    A summary of current guidance from creator-tool studies and platform spec guides. Most sources are marketing blogs, so treat exact percentages as directional. The engine's templates follow these rules.

    Face and emotion

    • Use one clear face with an obvious emotion that matches the video (surprise, worry, confidence).
    • One analysis reports about 6.8% CTR for thumbnails with faces against 4.1% without. Stronger expressions did better.
    • Keep the face big. At phone size a small face disappears.

    Words

    • Use 3–5 words. Thumbnails with 1–5 words beat ones with 11 or more.
    • Use a heavy sans font with a thick dark outline on a saturated background.
    • Don't repeat the title. Make the thumbnail raise a question that the title answers.

    Contrast and color

    • Use contrast in color and in idea: $1 vs $1,000,000, before vs after, big vs small.
    • Yellow, red and blue backgrounds dominate top channels. One accent color is enough.
    • Limit it to 2–3 main elements. Clutter disappears at small size.

    Platform rules

    • YouTube: keep the bottom-right clear for the duration badge. Test variants with Test & Compare, which judges by watch time.
    • Instagram: design Reel covers at 9:16, but the profile grid shows a centered 3:4 crop. Keep text in the middle.
    • TikTok: keep text away from the top bar, right-hand buttons and bottom caption.
